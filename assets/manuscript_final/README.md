# `assets/manuscript_final/` - authenticated manuscript-final rasters

This directory ships the exact raster images embedded in the final manuscript
candidate for the figures whose embedded bytes **cannot be regenerated by
running the code**. Each file here is byte-identical to the image stored
inside the manuscript `.docx` and its SHA-256 is recorded in `SHA256SUMS`
and in `docs/MANUSCRIPT_FIGURE_IDENTITY.csv`.

Two distinct situations are represented, and they are NOT the same claim.

## Figs. 5, 6 and 7 are no longer shipped here

`docs/MANUSCRIPT_FIGURE_IDENTITY.csv` records Figs. 5, 6 and 7 as
`materialization = reproduced_output` with no shipped asset: since the
weighted-centroid correction the manuscript embeds their **full-resolution
reproduced renders** directly, so no downscaled deployment export is needed.
The pre-correction exports that used to sit here are superseded. They were
relocated to the processed-data deposit under
`provenance_evidence/superseded_manuscript_assets/` in
`scorch_processed_data_v1.0.0.zip` (reserved data DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21717752,
reserved on an unpublished draft); `docs/RELOCATED_ARTIFACTS.csv` gives their
exact archive member paths, byte counts and hashes.

## `deployment_export_of_reproduced_original` - Fig. 9, 10, 11, A, C

The **plotted content is fully reproducible from the deposited data**: running
the producing script yields a full-resolution original that is byte-identical
to the approved original (see `approved_original_sha256` in
`docs/MANUSCRIPT_FIGURE_IDENTITY.csv` and `docs/FIGURE_PROVENANCE.csv`).
What is shipped here is the *downscaled deployment export* of that original -
the 1950 px-wide raster actually placed in the manuscript. The downscaling was
a one-off deployment-export step and is not part of the analysis, so those exact
bytes are frozen here rather than regenerated.

**Nothing scientific is frozen by this.** Re-run the pipeline and you get the
approved full-resolution original back, bit for bit.

Fig. 9 joined this class in the v1.0.0 pre-release correction: the
axial-orientation fix (doubled-angle axial event means replacing an invalid
arithmetic mean) regenerated its approved original end-to-end from the
deposit, and the shipped `Figure_09.png` is the deterministic width-1950
LANCZOS downscale produced by
`scripts/figures/common/make_manuscript_artwork.py`. The superseded
arithmetic-mean artifacts are archived under `legacy_defective_figure09/`
and are historical only.

## `manually_postprocessed_approved_artwork` - Fig. 8

For this figure the approved full-resolution original carries a manual
post-processing pass ("B2B-1") applied by the authors, which the code does not
reproduce. Three distinct artifacts exist and none is byte-identical to
another: the approved post-processed original, the deployment export shipped
here, and the script's own render.

The script's render reproduces the **plotted content only**. It ships as
a clearly-labelled *source component* under
`publication_outputs/figures/<figure>/source_components/` and must never be
presented as the publication figure. See `reproduction_note` in
`docs/MANUSCRIPT_FIGURE_IDENTITY.csv` for the measured pixel difference.

## What is deliberately NOT here

Fig. 1 and Fig. 4 are author-created slide exports with no runnable producer.
They ship as frozen approved artwork in `assets/frozen_figures/` and are
classified `frozen_approved_artwork`; regeneration from data is never claimed
for them.

Fig. 2, 3, 12, B, D and S.1 reproduce byte-identical to their manuscript embed
directly from the deposited data, so no frozen copy is needed or shipped.

## Reading `approved_original_sha256`

That column is copied verbatim from `docs/FIGURE_PROVENANCE.csv` and is not
always the same artifact as the manuscript embed:

* For the eight `deployment_export_*` figures it is the **approved
  full-resolution original**, of which the embed is a downscale.
* For Fig. 8 it is the **post-processed** approved original.
* For Fig. 1 and Fig. 4 it equals the embed - the frozen artwork *is* the
  approved original.
* For Fig. 2, Fig. 12 and Fig. D it records a **superseded** predecessor
  artifact that was replaced by an approved correction deployed on
  2026-07-30. Those three rows say so explicitly in `reproduction_note`, and
  the staging step refuses to ship a `data_generated` row whose
  `approved_original_sha256` differs from the embed without that
  documentation.

The only column that is ever safe to route on is `manuscript_final_sha256`.

## Routing

Consumers must select these files **by SHA-256**, using
`docs/MANUSCRIPT_FIGURE_IDENTITY.csv`. Historical filenames in this project
invert across eras (`figure5_*` is the current Fig. 8; `Fig11_*` is the current
Fig. 10; `New_Figure_S2_candidate` is the current Fig. D), so filename-based
routing is unsafe and is not used anywhere in the build.
